# Prompts de vídeo das histórias

Prompts para outra IA gerar um vídeo de 20 s por mapa, no estilo HQ/cordel, com os personagens do jogo. Cada vídeo tem 5 quadros de 4 s, os mesmos 5 quadros da HQ de introdução (`Stories.All`, `comic.<id>.1…5`). Os prompts estão em inglês (modelos de vídeo entendem melhor); as legendas ficam em português.

Direção de arte de referência: [`../ART_DIRECTION.md`](../ART_DIRECTION.md).

## Bloco de estilo e personagens (colar em todos os prompts)

```
STYLE: 20-second vertical (9:16) animated motion comic, hand-painted Brazilian
"cordel" woodcut look: thick, slightly irregular dark ink outlines (#2a1a12), flat
saturated colors with no gradients, aged paper texture (#f3e6c8), light hatching
only in shadows. Palette: sun yellow #f2b632, clay orange #b5532a, indigo blue
#2f5d8c, cactus green #4f7a3a, chita red #c8323c, night brown #1d1410. Warm
late-afternoon / oil-lamp light. Playful tone with a hint of a shiver, never
gory or scary.
FORMAT: 5 comic panels, 4 seconds each. Each panel has a thick ink border and a
paper-style caption box at the bottom with a hand-lettered Western/woodtype font
(like "Rye"). Transitions are a quick "page flip" or a panel slide. Motion inside
panels is limited and comic-like (parallax, slight zoom, characters bouncing,
speed lines), not realistic.
CHARACTERS (keep them identical in every panel):
- TIA UNICE: the oldest woman in the family, stern but loving. Long dress with
  small floral print, shawl, hair in a bun, wooden cane. Strong, recognizable
  silhouette. Chita red is her signature color.
- The COUSINS ("primos"): 4-5 kids from the Bahia countryside, each with a
  different skin tone and body shape. Chita-print shirts, button-up shirts,
  overalls. Hats: straw hat, cowboy leather hat, cap, headscarf. Barefoot or in
  flip-flops.
- MÁRCIO: the prankster cousin, leather cowboy hat, mischievous grin.
- VÓ (grandma): small, white hair, apron, appears at the door.
- RAG DOLL (boneca de pano): wool hair with a red ribbon, button eyes.
AUDIO: cheerful northeastern Brazilian forró: accordion, zabumba and triangle.
Light sound effects (kids' laughter, footsteps, a cane tapping). No narration;
the captions carry the story.
```

## 1. A Boneca (`doll`): casa da vó, dia, nostálgico e brincalhão

```
[STYLE + CHARACTERS BLOCK]
STORY: At Grandma's house in the Bahia countryside, Tia Unice's rag doll was an
untouchable treasure. Cousin Márcio dared the others to take it without her
seeing. The doll passed from hand to hand, Tia Unice noticed and stormed through
the house like thunder, and the cousins hid everywhere, giggling, until Grandma
shouted "Menino, almoçar!".
Cross-section view of a colorful countryside house (blue-tile floors, whitewashed
walls, wooden doors, clay roof), seen from above/side like a dollhouse.

PANEL 1 (0-4s) - KITCHEN: Cousins run around the big table with a chita
tablecloth, wood stove in the background. Caption: "Na casa da vó tinha mais
menino que um quartel."
PANEL 2 (4-8s) - AUNT'S BEDROOM: Tia Unice sits guarding the rag doll on an iron
bed with a chita bedspread. Márcio peeks from behind the door, grinning.
Caption: "No quarto da tia Unice morava uma boneca: o tesouro dela."
PANEL 3 (8-12s) - LIVING ROOM: Márcio points at the doll, then the doll flies
from hand to hand between cousins (motion trails). Caption: "Márcio desafiou:
quem pega a boneca sem a tia ver?"
PANEL 4 (12-16s) - DINING ROOM: Tia Unice appears furious in the doorway, cane
raised, red "!" over her head; cousins scatter and hide under the table, in the
wardrobe, behind the trunk, giggling. Caption: "Quando a tia deu por falta… foi
primo pra todo canto!"
PANEL 5 (16-20s) - Close up of Tia Unice with her eyes closed, counting on her
fingers, the doll peeking out from a hiding spot behind her. Caption: "Agora é
com você, tia: conte até zero e ache a boneca!"
Final frame: title card "A BONECA" on paper with a woodcut border.
```

## 2. O Lobisomem (`lobisomem`): quintal à noite, arrepio de brincadeira

```
[STYLE + CHARACTERS BLOCK]
ADJUSTMENT: this story happens at night. Use the night brown #1d1410 as the
background; the only warm light is the balcony of the house, in amber. Tone:
playful shiver, nervous laughter, nothing violent. The wolf is never shown, only
heard.
STORY: At Tia's backyard in the Bahia countryside (dry earth, coconut trees, a big
tree, a bush, a chicken coop, a toolshed, a water tank, a parked car, a flower
garden). An uncle told werewolf stories on the balcony to the cousins. Suddenly,
a wolf howl. Tia Unice screamed "lobisomem!", ran into the house and locked
everyone outside. The cousins ran and hid all over the yard.

PANEL 1 (0-4s) - BALCONY: Uncle (cape on his shoulders, lantern under his chin)
tells the story in a circle of 4 cousins, wide eyes. Only the balcony is warm
lit; the yard is black. Caption: "Noite no interior: o tio contava histórias de
lobisomem no quintal."
PANEL 2 (4-8s) - Yard in the dark, big full moon (a simple yellow disc). The
cousins freeze, look up. A "AUUUUUUU" lettered in woodcut style across the sky.
Caption: "De repente, no meio da escuridão, um uivo de lobo!"
PANEL 3 (8-12s) - Tia Unice with a terrified face, shawl flying, cane in the
air, running into the house shouting "LOBISOMEM!" in a speech bubble; two cousins
look at her in shock. Caption: "A tia Unice gritou 'lobisomem!' e correu pra casa."
PANEL 4 (12-16s) - The cousins banging on the closed wooden door, a key turning
from the inside. Caption: "Ela trancou a porta e deixou a gente lá fora!"
PANEL 5 (16-20s) - Wide shot of the yard: kids' eyes peeking out from behind the
barrel, coconut tree, water tank, flower bed, doghouse, car; a pair of glowing
eyes between the bushes at the back (playful, ambiguous). Caption: "Foi todo
mundo correr e se esconder. Agora o lobisomem é você!"
Final frame: title card "O LOBISOMEM".
```

## 3. A Rua da Vó (`rua`): dia, bagunça alegre

```
[STYLE + CHARACTERS BLOCK]
ADJUSTMENT: this story is daytime and has no scare at all. Bright sun, clear blue
sky, joyful energy.
STORY: In the small town square and street in front of Grandma's house, the
cousins gathered to play hide-and-seek. One counted and the others hid. Tia
Unice, out of nowhere, shouted where someone was hidden. Everybody ran back and
forth through the square and the street.
SETTING: two parallel streets (asphalt) around an island of trees with benches
and lamp posts, joining at one end and opening at the other into a wide paved
plaza with a church, a cross in the middle, a phone booth, a popcorn cart and a
trash bin. Houses are plain colorful facades, never entered.

PANEL 1 (0-4s) - In front of Grandma's gate (Grandma waves from the door): five
cousins gather, laughing. Caption: "Na frente da casa da vó, a criançada se
juntava pra brincar."
PANEL 2 (4-8s) - One cousin facing a wall with eyes covered, counting; the
others sprint away in different directions with dust clouds. Caption: "Um contava
de olhos fechados e todo mundo corria pra se esconder."
PANEL 3 (8-12s) - Aerial view of the square: kids dashing between trees, behind
the lamp posts, the popcorn cart, the church cross, the phone booth. Speed lines
and paths drawn as dotted lines. Caption: "Na praça e na rua, era gente correndo
pra lá e pra cá!"
PANEL 4 (12-16s) - Tia Unice on the sidewalk, one arm pointing, mouth wide open
with a big speech bubble "ELE TÁ ATRÁS DO POSTE!"; a cousin behind the lamp post
facepalms. Caption: "E a tia Unice, do nada, gritava onde alguém estava
escondido!"
PANEL 5 (16-20s) - Close-up of Tia Unice winking at the viewer, cousins peeking
from all around the frame. Caption: "Agora a tia Unice é você: ache a criançada!"
Final frame: title card "A RUA DA VÓ".
```

## Dicas para a IA de vídeo

- **Consistência:** gere primeiro uma imagem de referência de cada personagem (Unice, Márcio, a Vó e a boneca) e use-a como imagem de entrada em todos os clipes.
- **Mais controle:** se o modelo errar a sequência, peça um clipe de 4 s por quadro e junte os cinco no editor, com a legenda sobreposta depois (o texto em português costuma sair torto quando é gerado pela IA).
- **Texto na tela:** se a IA errar os acentos, apague as legendas do prompt e coloque-as depois com a fonte Rye, igual à do jogo.
